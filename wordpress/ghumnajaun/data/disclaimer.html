Information to help you plan an outing and understand the limits of our destination guides.

Using our guides

Ghumna Jaun publishes destination ideas, travel articles and information about places to eat, stay and visit. Use the guides as a starting point when planning your own trip.

Hours, entry fees, menus, facilities, road access and transport arrangements can change. Confirm details that affect your visit with the venue, transport provider or relevant authority before leaving.

Maps, routes and distances

Map pins and suggested stops help you explore an area. Distances labelled as straight-line estimates are approximate and differ from road distance and journey time.

A route link or listing of nearby petrol pumps and EV charging points does not establish current road conditions, fuel availability, charger compatibility or operating status. Check these details with the relevant provider.

Worship, heritage and outdoor visits

Follow local rules, signs and guidance from staff or community representatives. Access to shrines, heritage buildings and outdoor areas can change during festivals, maintenance or severe weather.

Choose activities that suit your group’s mobility, experience and circumstances. A category such as Family or Kids is a browsing aid. Check age limits, accessibility and supervision requirements at the destination.

Sources, history and photographs

Guides draw on the sources identified on the destination pages. Where a historical account is a local belief or legend, read it in that context. Photographs may show a place at an earlier date, and the site may crop them for display.

Photographer and license information is listed on the Photo Credits page. Permission to reuse an image depends on its source and applicable license.

Venues and travel operators

References to restaurants, stays and travel operators help you find providers to contact. Make arrangements directly with the provider and confirm pricing, availability, inclusions and cancellation terms.

Listings marked as samples illustrate a possible listing. Their menus and prices are examples rather than confirmed offers.

Corrections and questions

If a detail is out of date, please send the destination page link and the correction to hello@ghumnajaun.com. Supporting information from the venue or an official source helps us review it.
