About Ghumna Jaun

Find a place to go, and make a day of it.

A morning at a temple, lunch with a view, or an afternoon out with the kids. Ghumna Jaun brings nearby places together to help you choose your next outing.

We’re starting with Kathmandu Valley and the hills, towns and villages around it, from Bhaktapur’s old squares to the shores of Kulekhani.

A day out, your way

Some days call for a family picnic. Others are for a quiet walk together, a temple visit or a drive with friends. Browse by city or by the kind of outing you have in mind, including places for kids and restaurants with landscape views.

Details for the journey

A destination guide brings together things to do, visit information, bus and car guidance, and map links. Nearby stops and places to eat or stay help you shape the day around your own pace.

We also include food stops on the way, so the journey can be part of the outing.

How our guides come together

Our guides draw on tourism boards, municipal information, venue websites and other named sources. History sections link to their references, and photos link back to their sources on our Photo credits page.

Opening hours, fees and transport arrangements can change. Check the linked source or contact the venue when those details matter to your visit.

More stories from the road

We’re planning our own visits and YouTube vlogs to share a closer look at the places featured here. Alongside the destination guides, our travel articles offer ideas for putting a day together.
