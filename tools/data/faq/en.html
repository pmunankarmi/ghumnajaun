Answers to common questions about using Ghumna Jaun and planning your visit.

Does Ghumna Jaun arrange tours or accept bookings?

Ghumna Jaun is a travel information website. We do not currently arrange tours, sell entry tickets or accept hotel, restaurant or transport bookings. Contact the relevant venue or service provider directly for reservations.

How current are entry fees and opening hours?

Prices, opening hours, access rules and transport services can change. Our guides provide planning information, but you should confirm important details with the official venue or operator before leaving. Follow the source links included in each guide where available.

How can I report incorrect information?

Please contact us with the guide’s name or URL, the detail that needs correcting and a reliable source if available. We will review the information before updating the guide.

How can I suggest a destination?

Send your suggestion through our contact page. Include the place’s name, location and what makes it useful for a day trip, heritage walk or family outing. Suggestions are reviewed and do not guarantee publication.

Where can I find photo credits and reuse information?

Visit Photo credits for image sources and license information. Individual photos may have different owners and licenses. Check the original source and its license before reusing an image; the site’s copyright notice does not replace those licenses.

Are all destinations suitable for children or wheelchair users?

Suitability varies by place, route, weather and individual needs. Read the guide’s access and visit notes, and contact the venue about steps, paths, toilets and other facilities before travelling. A family category does not mean that every activity is suitable for every child.

Why do maps and videos ask for permission?

Some guides include maps and videos supplied by third parties. These load after you accept optional embedded content. You can change your choice using Cookie preferences in the footer. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
