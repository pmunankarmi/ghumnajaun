Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

How Ghumna Jaun handles messages, browser preferences and requests made while you use this website.

Who to contact

This policy describes the current Ghumna Jaun website. For privacy questions or requests about information you have shared with us, email hello@ghumnajaun.com.

Messages you choose to send

The Contact Us page asks for your name, email address, topic and message to send your enquiry directly through our website. Selecting “Send message” submits those details to our server for validation and email delivery.

Messages from the form are delivered to ghumnajaun@gmail.com. The direct “Get in touch” link addresses email to hello@ghumnajaun.com. Our hosting and email providers process form submissions to deliver your message. If you use the direct email link, your email provider also processes the message according to its own policy.

When you send a message, we receive the information in it and use it to answer your enquiry, review a correction, assess a suggestion or discuss a listing. Please include only information needed for that purpose.

Search, filters and location

Destination search and filters currently run in your browser. Search terms, selected cities, categories and starting points can appear in page URLs. When you open or share a URL, those values can be included in browser history, hosting logs or the shared link.

The starting-point selector uses the city or area you select. The current site does not request your device’s precise location. Opening a map or directions link takes you to the map provider, which may offer its own location features.

Cookies and browser storage

The site uses local storage to remember your selected light or dark theme under the name “ghumna-jaun-theme”. This is browser storage rather than a cookie. It stays on that browser until you change the setting or clear the stored site data.

Embedded OpenStreetMap maps and YouTube videos load after you choose Accept in the privacy banner or Cookie preferences. Choosing Reject keeps those embeds blocked. You can change this choice using Cookie preferences in the footer on any page.

We save your choice in local storage under “ghumna-jaun-consent” for up to 180 days. If storage is unavailable, your choice lasts for the current page visit. Rejecting later stops future embed loading. You may need browser controls to clear cookies or storage already set by a provider.

You can clear stored site data or adjust cookie controls in your browser. Blocking third-party resources may affect photos, fonts, maps or videos.

Embedded content and external services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, design resources from jsDelivr, and some photographs from Wikimedia and other credited sources. These resources load independently of the optional-embed choice. After acceptance, destination pages can embed OpenStreetMap maps and a YouTube video using the youtube-nocookie.com domain.

A request to an external service can share technical information such as your IP address, browser information and referrer information, depending on your browser settings. These services may process data outside Nepal.

YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embedding changes how viewing activity is used, but the player still connects to a third-party service. Your interaction with an embedded player or map is also governed by that provider’s policies.

For provider information, read Google’s Privacy Policy, the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy and jsDelivr’s Privacy Policy.

Technical records and service providers

Hosting and email services may process technical records needed to deliver the site or messages, maintain security and investigate faults. These records may include IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information and request times.

To limit contact-form abuse, the server temporarily stores a hash derived from your IP address and submission-attempt timestamps. These records count attempts during a 15-minute window and are eligible for periodic cleanup after 24 hours; they do not contain your message. We use providers as needed to operate the website and handle correspondence. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or necessary to address misuse or protect legal rights.

Keeping information and making requests

We keep correspondence while it is needed to respond to your enquiry, follow up on the matter or meet an applicable legal obligation. Email and hosting providers may retain records and backups under their own arrangements.

Contact hello@ghumnajaun.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have sent us. We may ask for enough information to confirm that a request relates to you and will consider it under applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our data handling changes, including when new contact forms, analytics, accounts or mobile app features are introduced. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
